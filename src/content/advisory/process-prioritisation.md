- **Top-down first.** Interviews with managers set the areas of interest, the goals and the priorities.
- **Then the people who do the work.** Interviews with operational teams collect pain points and needs for each process.
- **A first data and technical check** for each process, before anyone talks about solutions.
- **A ranking of processes** by value potential, as input for the use cases.
