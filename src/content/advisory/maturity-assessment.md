- **A structured assessment** of AI maturity across several dimensions, from strategy to data, skills and organisation.
- **Compared with a benchmark** built on years of research data, not with a generic checklist.
- **Barriers, not only scores.** Interviews show what blocks adoption and where the potential is.
- **Levers to act on**, taken from recent literature and from what peer organisations do.
