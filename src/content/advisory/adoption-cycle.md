- **The problem.** Many organisations use AI, feel faster, and still see no measurable return: the gain stays on the single task.
- **A cycle, not a launch.** Adoption is a loop that starts at the level of the task and grows to the level of the process.
- **Hidden effects are measured too**: over-reliance, rework and cognitive debt, next to time and quality.
- **Feedback across the whole process**, from who feeds the data to who checks the output.
- **New KPIs** that measure the value of the human-AI process end to end, not only the speed of one step.
