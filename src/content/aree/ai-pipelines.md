- **Speech to structure.** Transcription with speaker separation, then segmentation of a long recording into talks using the agenda.
- **Slides to agenda** with document OCR.
- **Audio extracted in the browser** with ffmpeg in a Web Worker: tens of megabytes uploaded instead of gigabytes.
- **Personalised reports** per participant, and exports to DOCX, Excel and the REFI-QDA exchange format, validated against its schema.
