- **Three levels**: dimension, category, code, each code linked to verbatim passages, in the spirit of the Gioia method.
- **Expected vs emerging themes**, kept separate on purpose.
- **AI-proposed merges** of similar codes, always confirmed by the researcher.
- **Saturation and novelty**: new codes per interview, AI share of the work, overlap with previous projects.
