- **Second opinion, not first draft.** The researcher proposes first; the AI then compares and suggests. Judgement stays with the person.
- **Provenance on every item.** Each code, theme or text carries a tag: AI, human-in-the-loop or human. It builds trust and makes AI work measurable.
- **The tool works with the AI off.** Every step can be done by hand; the AI is an accelerator, not a dependency.
- **One-click automatic run**, clearly labelled as automatic, for the cases where speed matters more than control.
