Standardized tests to evaluate the performance of AI models across various tasks and KPIs.

- SWE-Bench (Software Engineering Benchmark): It evaluates AI models’ ability to solve real-world software engineering problems by testing whether they can fix issues from open-source projects. Models receive a codebase and problem description, then they must generate code patches to pass unit tests.
- ARC-AGI (Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus for Artificial General Intelligence): This benchmark measures AI systems’ fluid intelligence through visual grid-based puzzles, testing abstract reasoning, pattern recognition, and the ability to generalize from minimal examples. The first version of this benchmark (ARC-AGI-1) was introduced in 2019, and a new edition has been launched in 2025, bringing a more challenging benchmark for evaluating state-of-the-art models
