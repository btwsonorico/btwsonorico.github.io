The Model Context Protocol is an open standard introduced by Anthropic in late 2024 to standardize how AI assistants and LLMs connect to external systems. It addresses the fragmentation of integrations in the current AI ecosystem by standardizing two-way communication for LLMs to dynamically access and interact with external tools, data sources, and services to perform information retrieval, tool calling and even actions. This protocol reduces the need for custom connections, such as APIs, to integrate each new AI model and external systems.
