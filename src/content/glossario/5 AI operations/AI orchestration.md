AI orchestration is the coordination and management of artificial intelligence models, systems and integrations. It covers effective deployment, implementation, integration and maintenance of the components in a greater AI system, workflow or app. Apart from AI models and AI agents, AI systems also include computational resources, data stores, and the data flows and pipelines that transmit data across an organization. Orchestration happens through three pillars: AI integration, AI automation and AI management (IBM).
