question: "What is the main risk of continual learning?"
answers:
  - "Forgetting what it already knew"
  - "Running out of data to read"
  - "Learning the same task too fast"
---
Training a model on new data over time without starting again. The main obstacle is catastrophic forgetting: learning something new can overwrite what the model already knew.
