level: medium
group: "Learning"
question: "What is the sign of overfitting?"
answers:
  - "Good on training data, poor on new data"
  - "Poor on training data, good on new data"
  - "Equally poor results on every kind of data"
---
A model overfits when it learns its training data too closely, noise included. It scores well on that data and poorly on new data. More data, simpler models and regularisation all reduce the problem.
