level: medium

question: "Where can a prompt injection hide?"
answers:
  - "In the content a model reads"
  - "In the chips of the server"
  - "In the weights of the model"
---
An attack that hides instructions in the content a model reads, such as a web page or an email. The model can follow them as if they came from the user. It matters most for agents, because they can act on what they read.
