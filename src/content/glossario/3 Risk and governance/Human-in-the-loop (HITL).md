Human-in-the-loop refers to a system or process in which a human actively participates in the operation, supervision or decision making of an automated system. In the context of AI, HITL implies that humans are involved at some point in the AI workflow to ensure accuracy, safety, accountability or ethical decision-making, while still achieving the benefits of automation (IBM).
