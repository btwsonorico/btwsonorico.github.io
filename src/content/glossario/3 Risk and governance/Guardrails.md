AI Guardrails are the safeguards that keep Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems operating safely, responsibly and within defined boundaries. These include policies, technical controls and monitoring mechanisms that govern how AI models generate outputs in real world situations. Guardrails are not one-off security controls: they span datasets, AI models, applications and workflows, making them foundational for enterprise-scale adoption and responsible AI practices (IBM).
