Hallucinations occur when an AI model generates a response which is grammatically correct, but factually incorrect or nonsensical. This phenomenon is a byproduct of the probabilistic nature of LLMs: they are predicting the next likely token based on patterns, not querying a database of verified facts. This can be prevented by grounding techniques and providing additional context.
