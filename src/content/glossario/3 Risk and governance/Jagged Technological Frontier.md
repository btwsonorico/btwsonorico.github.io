The Jagged Technological Frontier is a concept formalized in research by Harvard Business School and Boston Consulting Group (BCG). It describes the uneven capabilities of current AI systems, where tasks that seem equally difficult to humans, are performed with vastly different levels of competence by AI. When a task is inside the frontier of AI capabilities, using AI yields improvements in human performance, while when it is outside, it can lead to a significant decrease in quality and performance for humans. This “Jagged” nature implies that it is difficult to forecast AI performance without testing.
