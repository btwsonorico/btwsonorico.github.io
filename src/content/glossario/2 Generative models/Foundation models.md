These Generative AI models are basically general-purpose systems that can be adapted to a wide range of specific tasks without needing to be retrained from scratch. This adaptation happens through techniques such as fine tuning or simply prompt engineering, improving model performance on specific tasks without the need to build a model from scratch each time.
