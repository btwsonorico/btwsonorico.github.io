Multimodality is the capability of AI models to process and integrate information from multiple modalities or types of data. These modalities can include text, images, audio, video and other forms of sensory inputs.

- By analyzing different forms of data, multimodal systems are capable of receiving a more comprehensive understanding and generating more robust outputs, giving these models powerful capabilities and enabling more efficient interactions.
- Fundamentally, this paradigm is the fusion of all the input modalities and output modalities that humans are familiar with, and this has strong implications for providing additional context to these models, allowing businesses the ability to solve more ambitious problems.
