question: "What does closeness between two embeddings mean?"
answers:
  - "Their meanings are similar"
  - "Their lengths are identical"
  - "They come from the same file"
---
A list of numbers that represents the meaning of a piece of text, an image or another object. Similar meanings get nearby numbers, so software can compare meaning by measuring distance. Embeddings are the basis of semantic search and RAG.
