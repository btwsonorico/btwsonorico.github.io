A Small Language Model is an AI model capable of processing, understanding and generating natural language content. As the name implies, SLMs are smaller in scale and scope with respect to their counterpart Large Language Models.

- In terms of model size, SLMs parameters range from a few million to a few billion, as opposed to LLMs with hundreds of billions or even trillions of parameters, making SLMs more compact and efficient.
- SLMs represent a strategic opportunity for organizations, as they are ideal for resource-constrained environments, such as edge devices and mobile apps, or even for scenarios where AI inferencing needs to be handled locally.
