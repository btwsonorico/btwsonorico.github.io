Prompt engineering in generative AI is the process of crafting effective prompts to elicit desired outputs from large language models (LLMs). It's an iterative, test-driven process focused on refining inputs to achieve specific outcomes. This involves considering both the content and structure of the prompt to ensure accurate and high-quality responses. Effective prompt engineering is crucial for complex tasks, even though simpler tasks may not require it. (Google)
