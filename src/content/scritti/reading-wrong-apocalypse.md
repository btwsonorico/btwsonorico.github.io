## What it is

An essay by Andrea Pignataro, founder of ION, published during the February 2026 sell-off of software stocks. Two arguments:

- **Capability is not coordination.** Enterprise software does not exist to do cognitive work but to coordinate it, between people with different information, incentives and authority. An AI that can do a task does not replace the system around it: "organisations don't just use Salesforce, they speak Salesforce".
- **Individual rational adoption, collective self-destruction.** Each firm that adopts AI makes a rational choice. Together, they teach the platform the grammar of their whole industry.

## What I take from it

**It names a problem that is often underestimated and misunderstood.** The rules that made the cloud acceptable do not cover AI. With the cloud, the provider stores your data. With AI, data can be aggregated and analysed at many levels: not only what you send, but how you work, what you ask, what you correct.

**Companies are donating their grammar.** The way a firm structures its work is its competitive value. By adopting AI, firms hand it to the companies that will be able to replace them.

**Adoption is on the task, not on the process.** Today companies use AI on single tasks. The value flows out, towards the platform, and little comes back inside, because the process itself does not change.

**That is why open source and local inference will prevail.** If the grammar is the asset, the only safe way to use AI on it is with models you run yourself.
