## What it is

A Microsoft Research paper. It links the productivity losses of generative AI to the "ironies of automation", studied for decades in aviation and automated driving. Four causes:

1. People move from producing work to evaluating it.
2. Workflows are restructured in unhelpful ways.
3. Interruptions.
4. Easy tasks get easier, hard tasks get harder.

The proposed fixes come from human factors: continuous feedback, personalisation, interfaces designed around the task, clear task allocation.

## What I take from it

**Adoption problems are not new.** The literature on automation has studied them for a long time, since Bainbridge's "Ironies of automation" in 1983.

**With AI they matter more and more.** The person moves to evaluating outputs that are more and more abstract: not a number on a dial, but a report, an analysis, a plan. And they do it while interacting with many interfaces and systems at the same time, each with its own logic.
